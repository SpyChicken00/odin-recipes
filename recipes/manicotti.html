Manicotti

Description

Delicious peanut butter balls that don't need an oven! Easy to make for parties and minimal effort. You can add vanilla or m&ms to spice them up too.

Ingredients

  • Peanut Butter
  • Dry Oats
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Honey
  • Vanilla

Steps

  1. Put peanut butter, oats, honey, vanilla, and chocolate chips in a bowl.
  2. Mix together and add more ingredients to achieve desired ratio.
  3. Refrigerate until cold.
  4. Grab a spoonful of the mixture and form into ball shapes.
  5. Enjoy!
