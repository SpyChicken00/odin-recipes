Manicotti

Description

Delicious cheese-stuffed pasta covered in a tomato sauce that tastes even better as leftovers. Another one of my favorite foods from home. Note:recipe greatly simplified for time

Ingredients

  • Manicotti Pasta
  • Cheese Stuffing
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Parmasean
  • Parsely

Steps

  1. Boil manicotti pasta until flaccid.
  2. Prepare cheese stuffing and tomato sauce.
  3. Stuff manicotti pasta with cheese stuffing and place in dish.
  4. Cover with tomato sauce, parmasean cheese, and parsley.
  5. Cover pan with foil and bake in oven at 400 for 1 hour.
  6. Enjoy!
